Who we are

NewmanFrancis Community Impact CIC exists to build resilient communities and improve quality of life. We provide strategic support, funding and capacity-building services to the voluntary sector, charities, community groups and other organisations that deliver social impact, community empowerment and inclusive engagement.

We focus on places experiencing significant change, such as regeneration, redevelopment, building safety works or other major local developments, and on people experiencing social or economic disadvantage.

Grant programmes

Designing, managing and delivering community grants, including the NewFran Grants Programme.

Capacity building

Training, mentoring, workshops, planning support and bid-writing assistance for local groups.

Impact measurement

Simple, accessible tools and reports that show the difference projects make.

Who we support

People and communities across the United Kingdom, particularly those experiencing social or economic disadvantage, or the effects of change, disruption or transition in their neighbourhoods.

Community and voluntary groups

Local charities

Before you apply

Who can approach the CIC?

Community groups, residents' organisations, voluntary organisations and local charities working to improve housing, wellbeing, inclusion and quality of life, particularly in neighbourhoods experiencing regeneration or major change.

What does capacity-building support include?

Training, mentoring, workshops, planning support, bid-writing assistance and guidance on project design, governance, budgeting and monitoring.

Does the CIC hold money on behalf of our group?

No. The CIC does not act as a trustee or custodian of money belonging to others. Grants are awarded under a grant agreement, and programme funds are managed by the CIC in line with agreed budgets and delivery plans.

Can you help us apply to other funders?

Yes. We help groups identify suitable funding and sponsorship opportunities, meet funder requirements and prepare clear plans and budgets.
